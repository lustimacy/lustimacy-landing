Built for

Three kinds of curious

Lustimacy was designed from day one for the lifestyle community — couples in the scene, singles who play with couples, and adults exploring ethical non-monogamy on their own terms.

Couples in the lifestyle

Sign up as a couple from one account and share a single profile, or keep separate accounts and link up to three partners so your structure is visible before the first message. Either way, people see who you are with before they swipe — not after.

Open-minded singles

Filter for couples, singles, or both. See who's solo and who's got linked partners before you swipe. Detailed profiles tell you the lifestyle, the dynamic, and the energy before you ever start a chat.

Curious, careful, kind

New to the scene? Lustimacy is a low-pressure, judgment-free space to learn the language, ask questions, and meet people who'll meet you where you are. No tourists, no creeps, no rush.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Lustimacy available in my country?

Yes. Lustimacy is live on Google Play worldwide and free to join. We are concentrating on Germany and the Netherlands first, so those are the cities filling up fastest, but you can sign up from anywhere today. There is no iPhone app yet.

What does Lustimacy actually do for couples?

Two things. You can sign up as a couple and share one profile — both of your details live on it, and everyone sees you as a couple from the first swipe. Or, if you already have separate accounts or there are more than two of you, link up to three partners instead: each keeps their own login and swipes their own deck, with the connection shown on your profile. Singles can filter couples in or out, so nobody wastes anyone's time.

How do private photos work?

You upload private photos in your profile. Anyone who has not matched with you is only ever sent an irreversibly blurred copy — the original is withheld by our servers, not merely hidden by the app. The moment you match, the photos unlock automatically for both of you. There's no grant flow to manage.

Is Lustimacy free?

Yes. Sign-up, swipe, match and chat are free, and verification is free for everyone. A premium tier (Lustimacy Plus) adds priority visibility and "who liked you", similar to other dating apps.

How is my privacy protected?

We never link to your social media, never sell your data, and never make your profile crawlable by search engines. Verification photos are checked then discarded. You can delete your account in two taps and your data is permanently removed within 30 days.

What does ENM mean?

Ethical non-monogamy — relationships built on mutual awareness and consent. It includes the lifestyle, swinging, polyamory, open relationships, and everything in between. Lustimacy is a home for the whole spectrum.

Is this a hookup app?

Lustimacy is what you make of it. Some people are here for a weekend in Cap d'Agde; others are here to find a long-term play partner or a third for their relationship. The filters and detailed profiles let you find the speed and shape that fits you.

Can I browse other cities and countries?

Yes. In search filters you can set your search to your current location with a distance radius, to one or more specific countries, or go fully global. Handy before a trip, or if your scene is simply somewhere other than where you live.
